# International Student Admissions System App Flow Document

## Onboarding and Sign-In/Sign-Up
When a prospective international student first visits the admissions portal, they arrive at a welcoming landing page. This page introduces the university, outlines the application process, and offers clear calls to action for signing up or exploring as a guest. Applicants can choose to create an account with their email address or use a social login option if enabled by the university. The sign-up form asks for basic details such as name, email, and password, with a simple password strength indicator to help users pick a secure password.

After signing up, users receive a confirmation email. Once they verify their email address, they return to the portal and log in with their credentials. If they forget their password, they can click a "Forgot Password" link on the login page, submit their email address, and receive a password reset link. After setting a new password, they regain access to their account. Users can sign out at any time using the log out button in the header or sidebar, which clears their session and returns them to the landing page.

## Main Dashboard or Home Page
After signing in, applicants land on a personalized dashboard. At the top, a header greets them by name and displays their current application status. A navigation sidebar runs along the left side, listing links to the application form, status updates, FAQ, profile settings, and support. At the center of the page, a status widget shows a progress bar that highlights the current step of their multi-step application. Below this widget, quick action cards invite applicants to continue filling out their form, upload missing documents, or make a payment if required.

The header also includes a command palette icon that applicants can click to quickly jump to any page. If they click the application form link, they move directly into the form. If they choose the FAQ link, they see commonly asked questions. The sidebar remains visible on every page, ensuring that applicants can always navigate back to any major section of the portal.

## Detailed Feature Flows and Page Transitions
When an applicant begins the application, they click "Start Application" on the dashboard and enter the first section: Personal Information. This page displays a form where they enter name, date of birth, nationality, and contact details. Users click "Next" to move to the Education History section, where they add each school or university they attended, including dates and degrees. Validation messages appear if any required field is missing or formatted incorrectly.

After saving their education history, the user clicks "Next" to go to Document Upload. Here, they upload scanned copies of transcripts, passports, and language certificates. Each upload shows a progress indicator, and if an upload fails, an error message appears with instructions to retry. Once all required documents are uploaded successfully, the user clicks "Review and Submit." On the review page, they see all entered information and uploaded files. If they notice any mistakes, they can click the edit icon next to each section, which takes them back to that form page.

When the user confirms that their application is complete, they click "Submit Application." This action triggers an email notification to them and creates a pending application record in Supabase. The user is then prompted to pay the application fee via Stripe. They enter payment details on a secure checkout page provided by Stripe Elements. After successful payment, a webhook updates their application status to "Fee Paid" in the database. The applicant is then redirected back to the dashboard, where the status widget now reflects that payment is complete and the application is under review.

Administrators access a separate admin dashboard by navigating to the `/admin` route. They authenticate with their staff credentials, and role-based rules ensure only authorized staff can enter. On the admin dashboard, staff see a list of incoming applications with filters for status, nationality, and submission date. Clicking an applicant’s row opens a detail view where staff can review information, view uploaded documents, and update the application status to "Under Review," "Accepted," or "Rejected." When staff update status, Supabase real-time listeners push the change to the applicant’s dashboard without needing a page refresh.

## Settings and Account Management
Applicants can manage their personal account settings by clicking "Profile Settings" in the sidebar. On this page, they update their name, email, and password. They can also configure notification preferences, choosing to receive emails for status changes or reminders about incomplete sections. Any change they make is validated and saved immediately, and a confirmation message appears when the update succeeds.

If applicants need to update billing information for payment retries, they return to the application fee page on the dashboard. Here, they can re-enter or update their card details before proceeding with payment. Once billing is successful, they return to the dashboard and see the updated status.

To navigate back to their application or any other part of the portal, users simply click items in the sidebar or use the command palette. This keeps their experience fluid, without losing the context of where they left off.

## Error States and Alternate Paths
If applicants enter invalid data, such as an improperly formatted date of birth or an incomplete address, inline error messages appear beneath the offending fields. This prevents them from moving forward until the data is corrected. During file uploads, if the user loses connectivity or a file exceeds size limits, a clear error message displays, and they can retry the upload once connectivity is restored.

If the payment fails, the Stripe checkout page shows the reason—such as a declined card—and offers the user a chance to try a different card. If a webhook fails to confirm the payment status, the system shows a pending state on the dashboard and automatically retries the webhook in the background.

Applicants who do not finish their application in one session can click "Save and Exit." Their progress is stored in the database, and when they return, the dashboard invites them to pick up where they left off. If they attempt to access an admin route without proper authorization, they see an access denied page and a link back to the main portal.

## Conclusion and Overall App Journey
From the first moment an international student lands on the admissions portal, they are guided through a clear and supportive process. They create an account, verify their email, and log in to access a dashboard that keeps them informed of their progress. The multi-step application form walks them through entering personal information, academic history, and document uploads, with robust validation and error handling at each step. Secure payment integration ensures the application fee is collected reliably, with real-time status updates reflecting every change. Behind the scenes, university staff manage and review applications through a dedicated admin interface, updating statuses that immediately appear on the student’s dashboard. Throughout their journey, applicants can manage account settings, recover lost passwords, and resume incomplete forms. This cohesive flow ensures that international students can complete their applications smoothly and stay informed from start to finish.
